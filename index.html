Bahand Abdulrahman

Biomedical Engineering Student at the University of Cincinnati

I'm a Biomedical Engineering student at the University of Cincinnati College of Engineering and Applied Science (CEAS), with a passion for building the next generation of scientific instruments and prosthetics.

I'm the founder of EagleVision, a phone mount and web app that streams one microscope to every student's device, which I now run as an independent venture with my co-founders. During high school, I was Lead Programmer for FTC Robotics Team 6133 "The NUTS!", President of the WHHS INTERalliance / Programming Club, and a Teaching Assistant for the first online Arduino course for students in Kurdistan.
