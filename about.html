About Me

Who I Am

I'm a Biomedical Engineering student at the University of Cincinnati College of Engineering and Applied Science (CEAS), class of 2031. I got hooked on how cells work in 9th grade, reading about proteins and membranes in college textbooks, and that is what pulled me toward BME over a straight computer science or robotics path.

Long term, I want to work in bionics or biomechatronics, building scientific instruments and prosthetics that help people.

What I'm Working On

EagleVision Microscopy (eaglevision.dev): Most classrooms share one microscope, so each student gets a few seconds at the eyepiece. I started EagleVision in April 2025 to fix that. It's a 3D-printed phone mount (under $3 to produce) and a web app that streams one microscope to every student's device. I built all of the software and hardware. We piloted it at Madeira Elementary with 80+ students, filed a provisional patent, and won Best of the Best at the 2026 UC IT Expo (high school division). I now lead a 3-person team with co-founders Daiveion Greenwade and Sarah Hu, and we're working with UC Venture Lab.

Applied Autonomy Lab, UC: I'm the only freshman mentee on a senior design team that uses sound and vibration data to train AI to detect faults in drones. I designed the aluminum extrusion test rig in CAD, the mounts that hold the drone to it, and a microphone holder. I also helped solder the sensors, wired them, and worked alongside the team to create the data-collection software (two ESP32 boards and a Python live viewer).

Khidmah (khidmah.bahandab.com): I've been a general volunteer at the Islamic Association of Cincinnati (Clifton Mosque) since 2023, helping out with whatever they need. Volunteering there was split across separate WhatsApp groups with no onboarding, so I proposed and built Khidmah, a volunteer system with registration, onboarding, a shared calendar, volunteer matching, and hour tracking.

Arabic Pronunciation Instructor: I teach a weekly online Arabic pronunciation class for Kurdish students, and I'm rebuilding the course into my own slides and quizzes.
